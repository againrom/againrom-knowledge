### UNIT-AREAHP-073

**Each reached Building direct-damage call independently computes and writes HP; its rectangle is not a normalization divisor.** `R0492+1c -> R1827` returns 1, so spell-2's two size calls divide its copied damage bytes by 1, regardless of `+60/+61` or attached-cell count. `R0564` takes the non-unit / Building virtual-predicate arm, calls `R0653` and subtracts the returned damage as a word from `+42`, then clamps negative signed HP to zero; positive damage notifies. The core retains `UNIT-STRUCTDAMAGE-064`'s spread-positive gate and `max(base+U[0,spread]-5,0)`, independently per call. Neither body refuses current HP zero. With a fixed nonempty slot and empty dependent collection, original-instruction probes give `100 -> 94,88,82,76,70,64,58,52,46` across nine calls (base10/spread1/roll1), and `10 -> 4,0,0,0` across four. | **High** for local dispatch, literal divisor, arithmetic/width/order and conditional repeated calls. ### UNIT-STRUCTDETACH-074

**The reached Building destructor clears the current mask walk, not a saved list of successfully attached cells.** `R1833` calls `R1358` when world is non-null. Detach uses the same dimensions, mask and row-major keys; if a selected cell has no record it returns zero immediately. If the record exists it clears payload `+0c` without comparing that pointer with the destructed object, writes the record and recomputes; an otherwise empty payload can restore baseline planes and remove the record. Thus a partial registration does not itself supply rollback ownership: conditional on reaching an occupied collision cell during later detach, that slot is cleared even if it references another object. | **High** for the complete local loop, pointer-free unconditional clear and absent record exit. **Unknown** for a live partially registered object's destructor reach, the HP-to-destructor transition, and whether outer world ordering permits the conditional collision-clear case. No claim that lethal area damage immediately detaches or that structures persist forever.
