# Claim registry — HERO (character generation and the derived-stat graph)

Level 2 ledger. Index: [registry.md](registry.md) · spec: [`formats/hero/format.md`](../formats/hero/format.md). Legend: `✔ promoted` (also in the spec) · `● active` · `✖ retracted`. IDs are permanent.

Not a file format — the **simulation area** that turns a player's four numbers into a hero. It sits on
top of the placeable-definition database (`DAT-ACT-006` gives the actor its `Data.bin` row and the
field map `+0x84…+0x134`) and feeds the mover (`TERR-MOVE-056`). Opened by
[EXP-0063](../experiments/EXP-0063-hero-stats/), which read every routine below at instruction level.
The **spell** side of the same actor is [`magic.md`](magic.md); two rows here
(`HERO-REGEN-021`, `HERO-DAMAGE-022`) came out of that round and stayed in this ledger because their
subject is the actor's own fields.

Vocabulary is the **engine's own** wherever the image supplies a name: `Body` / `Reaction` / `Mind` /
`Spirit` are `scenario\npc.reg` key literals, the six skills are `Data.bin` column titles. Where the
image supplies no name the field is described, not christened.

Two integer conventions hold throughout and are stated once here rather than in every row:
`__ftol` (`R0279`) sets x87 rounding-control to **11, truncate toward zero**, and `IDIV` and the
`CDQ`/`SUB`/`SAR 1` idiom truncate toward zero as well. The **only** rounding-to-nearest in the whole
area is `R0824`'s `+0.5` before its truncate.
