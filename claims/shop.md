# Claim registry — SHOP (the shop: stock, price, trade)

Level 2 ledger. Index: [registry.md](registry.md) · spec: [`formats/shop/format.md`](../formats/shop/format.md). Legend: `✔ promoted` (also in the spec) · `● active` · `✖ retracted`. IDs are permanent.

Not a file format — the **simulation area** that turns a placed structure into a place you can
trade at. It sits on `ALM-CLS-037` (which type-4 records take the structure arm), on
`DAT-OBJ-002`/`DAT-SCHEMA-004` (the `Data.bin` item collections the stock is drawn from) and
on `REG-SCN-059` (the campaign's per-mission keys). Opened by
[EXP-0057](../experiments/EXP-0057-shop/), which read the class, the generator and both commit
paths at instruction level; the listings are `EXP-0057/evidence/rom-shop-excerpt.md` and the
placement census is `EXP-0057/evidence/shop-placements.csv`. Continued by
[EXP-0058](../experiments/EXP-0058-shop-lifecycle/), which read the **lifecycle** — when the stock is
built, cleared and restocked, whether it is saved, where the seed comes from, and which shop a
session can reach — and corrected two of EXP-0057's readings in the process. Closed on the campaign
side by [EXP-0059](../experiments/EXP-0059-shop-mission/), which traced the **town shop's only
per-mission input** from `scenario.reg` to the generator's `max` and measured what that ceiling
admits. The **trade half** — the shop screen, the table, the two commit buttons, the move command and
what a duplicate is — is [EXP-0061](../experiments/EXP-0061-shop-trade/); its listings are
`EXP-0061/evidence/rom-shop-trade-excerpt.md` and its measured screen geometry is
`EXP-0061/evidence/shop-screen-grid.json`.

> **Owner testimony appears in this ledger only as an `Origin:` note, never in a Confidence cell.**
> The owner's recollection of playing his own install is a third evidence class: it may raise a
> question, aim a search and bound a claim, and it may never enter or raise a grade. It is recorded
> verbatim in `EXP-0058/evidence/owner-testimony.md`, and the rule is normative in
> `METHODOLOGY.md` → *A third evidence class*. Where the image contradicts it, the image wins.

### SHOP-ANIMATION-081

**Shop-panel progression is inside paint, gated by unsigned elapsed time >=100 ms, not one advance per paint or elapsed-time catch-up.** `R1539`, merchant vtable `L09658+2c`, first requires `view+148 != 0`. Its eligible block calls `L09659` for sound, replaces global stamp `L09660` with a new `timeGetTime` result, advances enabled racks, then at most one merchant mode. The first stamp initialization is `now-100`. Idle eligibility separately compares `now-L09661` with a newly computed `5000+1000*(rand()%5)` on each eligible pass, even while a merchant mode prevents arming; completion refreshes that second stamp. Both stamps are static globals, not per-rack counters. This establishes local paint order and candidate thresholds, not guaranteed visible 10 fps or a sampled-once uniform wait.

**Confidence.** High for this function's branch/order/arithmetic; outer dispatch, wall-clock presentation and original-runtime witness Unknown.

**Recorded status.** ✔ promoted

**Evidence.** [EXP-0301](../experiments/EXP-0301-shop-interior-animation/)

### SHOP-ANIMATION-082

**Each selected rack loads eleven files but its normal loop draws indices3..8, files4..9.** Selector `R1523(i)` loads folder `4-i` through `R1527`, enables bit `1<<i`, and resets its index at `panel+244+4*i` to 0. A different previous rack is assigned index9. `L09662` increments once, maps 9 to 3, and at 10 clears that rack bit and frees its files before drawing. Thus file10 is only a possible inter-step closing draw; file11 is not drawn by this normal progression. Same-shelf selection returns without work. Entry `R0705` selects 0, folder04. Input `R1526` selects another hit rack and additionally arms Yes. Four folders, each eleven BMPs, exist on both roots with equal per-file hashes; no claim that every loaded file visibly animates follows from that inventory.

**Confidence.** High for local selector/advance/draw relation; Medium for resource census; visible first/closing frames depend on paint timing and are Unknown.

**Recorded status.** ✔ promoted

**Evidence.** [EXP-0301](../experiments/EXP-0301-shop-interior-animation/)

### SHOP-ANIMATION-083

**The merchant has a static base and three priority-ordered draw modes, not one loop over all files.** Base `Pose2-3/1.bmp` is `panel+128`. Idle bit10 uses the replaceable bitmap at+12c; `L09663` increments shared index+254 modulo30 and loads file `index+1`. From index0, uninterrupted idle draws files2..28; step28 loads29 then clears idle and resets the index before drawing. Yes bit20 and No bit40 use twelve-slot arrays at+1e0/+210: slot0 is the base pose, slots1..11 load Yes/No files2..12. ### SHOP-ANIMATION-084

**Reaction requests add mode bits without restarting the shared merchant index.** Sell-with-items and affordable-buy helpers load Yes and OR20; unaffordable buy loads No and OR40; rack-click selection OR20. These trigger bodies neither clear other merchant bits nor reset+254. Paint both advances and draws with priority idle10, Yes20, No40, so overlapping bits can defer a reaction. Constructors `R1524`/`R1699` zero+254; ordinary mode completion also zeros it. Exit `R1700` disables view+148, clears flags and calls static/animation cleanup; entry reloads resources and resets rack selection, but neither inspected entry/exit nor cleanup resets+254 or the static clock stamps. This is not proof that a reused live panel retains a particular index: inherited transitions, object reuse and interruption outcomes were not witnessed.

**Confidence.** High for the named trigger/cleanup bodies and priority; Medium for lifecycle inference; runtime re-entry, malformed retained-index behavior and visible interruption remain Unknown.

**Recorded status.** ✔ promoted

### SHOP-CONSUME-073

**Shelf spell subtypes and price/power identities are explicit and distinct.** `R1036` constructs `Book_Fire/Water/Air/Earth/Astral` through `R2076`, appends fresh Effect kind 42 with spell id at Effect+40, and sets Item+1c from Spells parameter 21. Its second loop constructs `Scroll_*`, appends kind 41, takes parameter20 as base price, admits that base within inclusive min/max, sets `n=min(100,10*floor(max/base))`, draws power1..n through `R1715`, writes it to **Effect+42**, and sets stored Item price to `base*(floor(power/10)+1)`. The scaled result is not re-tested against max. Both candidate loops run ids1..27; Book additionally excludes11,17,27 and has no school constructor for boundary ids1,6,12,18, leaving ids2..5,7..10,13..16,19..26. Slow28 is outside both loops. This is not a stack-quantity law. At release `R0003` reads signed Effect+42 without the book clamp and passes its low byte to `R0625`; ordinary generated powers are 1..100.

**Confidence.** High; complete constructor, admission, assignment and release distinguish Item count from Effect power and label both subtypes.

**Recorded status.** ✔ promoted

**Evidence.** [EXP-0275](../experiments/EXP-0275-consumable-use/)

### SHOP-CONSUME-074

**Actual shelf quantities come from a later draw and six literal-Potion additions.** `R1500` calls `R1036`, then `R1711(rand1..8,1,shop+68)`. `R1751` (left): returns when `[this+0x84] == 0` or `[[this+0x90]] == 0` (`L10008`, `L10009`); otherwise shifts the per-cell arrays at `[this+0x60]` and `[this+0x2098]` by one cell, decrements `[[this+0x90]]` (`L10010`) and calls `L09985`. `R1752` (right): returns when `[this+0x84] == 0`, or when `[[this+0x84]+8] <= [[this+0x90]] + [this+0x88] * [this+0x8c]` (`L10011`, `L10012`, `L10013`); otherwise shifts them the other way, increments `[[this+0x90]]` (`L10014`) and calls `L09985`. Both are guards, not assignments: neither stores a bound into the base.

For the 5x1 strip, `[this+0x88] * [this+0x8c] = 5`. With 5 or fewer elements the right guard never passes, and the left guard refuses at base 0, so both clicks leave the base unchanged. With more than 5 the base moves in `0..count-5` through these two steps: the right step passes while `count > base + 5` and stops at `base = count - 5`. A base already above `count - 5` is not lowered by the right step; only the left step lowers it, one cell per click. The left guard tests for exactly 0, so a negative base would not be refused; no negative value was found among these steps.

**Confidence.** High for the two step bodies and their guards (operands in `evidence/strip-listing-excerpt.txt`). Medium for the range `0..count-5` as an invariant: it holds for every base reached through these two steps, but the other writers of the member field that `[this+0x90]` points at (`member+0xdc`, `SHOP-PICKER-043`) were not enumerated. The two step routines are shared: `callto` finds 12 call sites each in five owners (`L10015`, `R1749`, `R1750`, `L10016`, `L10005`), so their guards bind other grids too.

**Unknown.** Writers of `member+0xdc` outside the shop strip, in particular after an element is removed from the member's container while the shop is open.
