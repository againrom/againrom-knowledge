# Claim registry — SPR16A (.16a / .16 sprite)

Level 2 ledger. Index: [registry.md](registry.md) · spec: [`formats/spr16a/format.md`](../formats/spr16a/format.md). Legend: `✔ promoted` (also in the spec) · `● active` · `✖ retracted`. IDs are permanent.

> **Confidence re-graded by the standing sweep (2026-07-25).** This ledger already paid for the
> confidence rule — four of the repo's overturns are its retracted rows. What survived is
> EXP-0015's instruction-level work, which also retro-fits a discriminator to `SPR16A-RLE-002`.
> One demotion, and it is a *repeat*: `SPR16A-STRUCT-001` still says the frame count is "not
> stored", which is what `.256`'s ledger said before EXP-0013 found it in the 4-byte trailer.
>
> **2026-07-28 — the flag was right (EXP-0042).** The count *is* stored, in the same place
> `.256` stores it: `SPR16A-TRLR-012`, 545/545. The "not stored" clause is withdrawn from
> `SPR16A-STRUCT-001` and recorded in [`retracted.md`](retracted.md). EXP-0042 also closed the
> `.16` font glyph grammar and found that **five** font atlases ship, not three. It had no
> `rom.exe` access (a parallel experiment held the Ghidra project), so its interpretive clauses
> are Medium by the confidence rule and each names the reader-side check that would lift it.
>
> **2026-07-29 (later the same day) — the install moved under the ledger, and the high half is
> read (EXP-0050).** A GOG update replaced the EN data with the **Russian release** at 16:41 and
> the owner reinstalled EN at 16:56 (the reinstalled `graphics.res` re-hashes exactly to the
> `3ec2f34c…` pin; the RU state is preserved at `gameversions\ru`). Every corpus figure in this
> ledger recorded before that date describes the EN archive — which for the SPR16A domain turns
> out to matter for **one node only**: `font2.16` (`SPR16A-FONT-022`). The headline: the atlases'
> reachable records 96..223 carry **the whole Russian alphabet in a hybrid arrangement**
> (А..Я `0xB0..0xCF`, а..п `0xD0..0xDF`, р..я `0xF0..0xFF`, beside CP437's accented Latin at
> CP437's own positions) — in the **English** build's bytes too (`SPR16A-FONT-020`) — and the
> dead tails are older in-place-overwrite layers of the same font (`SPR16A-FONT-021`).
>
> **2026-07-29 — the reader was read (EXP-0047), and every check EXP-0042 named came back.**
> Four of those Mediums are lifted and one Unknown is answered against the analogy that would
> have been assumed. The headline is structural: **there are two loaders, not one.** `.16a` is
> parsed by the *same constructor as `.256`* (`R0753`) — so `SPR256-TRLR-021`'s `AND
> 0x7fffffff` and its bit-31 palette gate are, instruction for instruction, the `.16a`'s — while
> `.16` has its own (`R1511`), which takes the trailing `u32` **raw** and can never read a
> palette. That is what the 542/3 corpus partition could not cut and the binary does.
