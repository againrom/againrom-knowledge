# Claim registry — PAL (.pal palette)

Level 2 ledger. Index: [registry.md](registry.md) · spec: [`formats/pal/format.md`](../formats/pal/format.md). Legend: `✔ promoted` (also in the spec) · `● active` · `✖ retracted`. IDs are permanent.

> `.pal` is not a container of its own: it is a **BMP** whose 256-entry colour table the engine
> reads and whose pixel data it never opens. It matters because it is the whole of the per-tier
> and per-owner recolour — there is no arithmetic anywhere in that path.
